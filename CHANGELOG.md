## 0.1.0

- `Murkio.initialize` with only the publishable key; session in the secure storage,
  single-flight refresh, `getToken`, `getUser`, `signOut`, `MurkioHttpClient`.
- `MurkioIdentityCanvas`: the published login canvas with the sign-in, signup, email code,
  two-step verification, password reset and social sign-in flows.
- Standalone widgets: email input, password input, keep-logged, login and register
  buttons, error, `MurkioIdentityForm` and `MurkioAuthBuilder`.
